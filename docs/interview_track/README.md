# Senior Interview Track

## Why this exists

The core course is excellent for learning C++, but senior interviews at competitive firms also require:
- timed coding performance
- system design
- behavioral / leadership communication
- debugging under observation
- project deep dives

This track closes that gap.

## The four interview pillars

1. Coding interviews
2. System design
3. Behavioral / leadership
4. Project deep dives

## Weekly overlay

### Stage 1
- 2 easy coding interview problems per week
- 1 explanation drill out loud

### Stage 2
- 2 medium problems per week
- 1 timed 35-minute coding session per week
- 1 light design drill per week

### Stage 3
- 3 interview problems per week
- 1 design session every 2 weeks
- 1 behavioral/story session every 2 weeks
- 1 mock interview every 2 weeks

## Senior readiness standard

You should be able to:
- solve medium interview problems consistently
- communicate tradeoffs while coding
- lead a 35–45 minute system design conversation
- tell credible stories about ownership, ambiguity, conflict, failure, and impact
- discuss at least 2 major projects deeply
