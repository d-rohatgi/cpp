# 14-Week Interview Overlay

Use this alongside the main course.

## Weeks 1–2
- 2 easy coding problems each week
- 1 explain-your-code drill each week

## Weeks 3–6
- 2 easy + 1 medium each week
- 1 behavioral story draft every 2 weeks

## Weeks 7–10
- 2 medium problems each week
- 1 timed session each week
- 1 light system design each week

## Weeks 11–14
- 2 medium + 1 medium/hard each week
- 1 mock coding interview every 2 weeks
- 1 deeper system design every 2 weeks
- 1 project deep dive every 2 weeks
