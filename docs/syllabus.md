# Redesigned Syllabus

## Why this redesign is better
The fastest path to elite is not hardest material first.
It is:
1. fluency
2. fundamentals
3. systems depth

## Pacing
- 24 weeks total
- one main assignment per week
- early weeks prioritize comfort and completion
- middle weeks prioritize invariants and tests
- late weeks prioritize performance, architecture, and discipline
